DocLens Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

DocLens processes document images and recognized text on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your documents, OCR text, search queries, or purchase history. We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking, or sell personal information.

Camera and photo-library access are used only when you choose to scan or import a document. Your files remain under your control in the app and can be removed from the app by you.

Apple processes App Store purchases and subscriptions under its own privacy policy. DocLens receives only the entitlement information necessary to unlock Pro features.

For support or privacy questions, visit DocLens Support.
