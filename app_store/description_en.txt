DocLens turns your camera into a powerful AI-powered document reader.

Point your camera at any document — a receipt, contract, handwritten note, article, or ID — and DocLens extracts the text and generates an instant AI summary. No more squinting, typing, or hunting for a specific line.

── WHAT DOCLENS DOES ──

📷 SCAN
Use the built-in document camera for crisp, multi-page scans. Or import any existing image from your photo library.

🔍 OCR
High-accuracy text recognition powered by Apple Vision framework — works offline, supports 7 languages including English and Japanese.

✨ AI ANALYSIS
After scanning, AI automatically produces:
• A clear document summary
• Key facts and figures extracted
• Smart category detection (receipt, contract, note, article, and more)

📂 ORGANIZE
Your document library with search and category filters. Tap any document to view the original scan, extracted text, and AI summary side by side.

↗️ EXPORT
Share extracted text or summaries via the system share sheet — copy to clipboard, send via email, or export to any app.
