DocLens turns your camera into a private document scanner.

Point your camera at a receipt, contract, handwritten note, or article. DocLens scans it, extracts Japanese or English text on your device, and creates a concise document overview.

WHAT DOCLENS DOES

SCAN
Use the built-in document camera for crisp, multi-page scans. Or import any existing image from your photo library.

OCR
On-device text recognition for Japanese and English. Your scan stays on your device.

DOCUMENT OVERVIEW
After scanning, DocLens helps you review a short overview, key lines, and a practical document category.

ORGANIZE
Your document library with search and category filters. Tap any document to view the original scan, extracted text, and overview side by side.

EXPORT
Share extracted text or summaries via the system share sheet - copy to clipboard, send via email, or export to any app.
