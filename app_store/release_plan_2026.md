## Store copy

### English

**Subtitle:** Scan documents. Find what matters.

**Description:** DocLens turns the documents you choose into a clean, searchable library. Capture a page or import an image, review the extracted text, then keep a PDF-ready record for later. Text recognition and document overviews run on your device. There is no account, no advertising SDK, and no document upload.

**Keywords:** document scanner,ocr,pdf,receipt,contract,text scan,organizer

## Review notes

No login is required. Tap Scan or Import, choose a document, then review the extracted text and export a PDF. Camera and photo access are used only for user-selected documents. The app does not transmit document images or text. The annual subscription has a seven-day trial; the monthly plan has no trial. Restore Purchases is available on the paywall.
